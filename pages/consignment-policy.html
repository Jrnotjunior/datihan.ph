Consignment Policy.

The basic framework for consignors who entrust items to DATIHAN.PH.

1. Consignment arrangement

Consignment means the item owner authorizes DATIHAN.PH to market and sell an accepted item on the owner's behalf under the agreed arrangement.

2. Item acceptance

Items are subject to review before acceptance. We may decline items that do not fit the shop's current categories, condition standards, authenticity requirements, or available inventory needs.

3. Condition and disclosure

Consignors should disclose known defects, alterations, stains, repairs, authenticity concerns, and other material information. DATIHAN.PH may inspect and document accepted items for listing and marketing.

4. Pricing and sale

The listing price, selling period, and consignor share should be agreed before an item is placed for sale. DATIHAN.PH may recommend or adjust pricing only according to the arrangement agreed with the consignor.

5. Proceeds and payout

For an item that sells, the consignor's payout is calculated according to the agreed consignment terms. Payout timing and method will be communicated as part of the arrangement.

6. Unsold items

When the agreed selling period ends, the consignor may be asked to collect the item or agree to an updated arrangement. Specific handling of unclaimed items should be stated in the applicable consignment agreement.

7. Returns and customer issues

If a customer raises an issue concerning an item, DATIHAN.PH may review the listing, item condition, transaction, and available evidence before determining an appropriate resolution under the applicable policies.

8. Agreement-specific terms

Where a signed or otherwise confirmed consignment agreement contains terms that differ from this general policy, the agreed consignment terms govern the specific item or arrangement.
