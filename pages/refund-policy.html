Refund Policy.

Our approach to returns, refunds, and issues with pre-loved items.

1. All sales are final

Because DATIHAN.PH sells curated pre-loved items, purchases are generally final. Each item is sold as-is unless a different condition is clearly stated on the product page or agreed in writing before purchase.

2. Item condition

We make reasonable efforts to describe and photograph condition, measurements, defects, stains, signs of wear, and other material details. Customers are encouraged to review the listing carefully before ordering.

3. When to contact us

If an item has a material issue that was not disclosed in its listing, contact DATIHAN.PH as soon as possible after delivery and provide your order details and clear photos of the issue. We will review the concern and determine the appropriate resolution.

4. Approved refunds

If DATIHAN.PH approves a refund, the amount and method will be confirmed with the customer. Refunds may be limited to the affected item and may depend on the circumstances of the order.

5. Shipping and delivery issues

For damaged parcels, missing items, or other delivery problems, keep the packaging and supporting evidence and contact us promptly so the order can be reviewed with the relevant delivery provider.

6. Questions

For refund questions, use the Contact page or the support channel provided with your order.
