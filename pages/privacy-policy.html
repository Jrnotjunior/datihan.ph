Privacy Policy.

How DATIHAN.PH handles information used to operate the store.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect information such as your name, email address, contact details, delivery address, account information, order details, and messages you send to us.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, process orders, communicate with customers, arrange delivery, provide support, maintain website security, and improve our services.

3. Payment information

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. DATIHAN.PH does not need to store full payment-card credentials when those credentials are processed directly by the payment provider.

4. Service providers

We may use trusted technology and delivery providers to operate the website, authenticate accounts, store necessary data, process payments, and fulfill orders. These providers receive only information needed for their service, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Cookies and local storage

The website may use browser storage and similar technologies for functions such as maintaining shopping-cart state, preferences, authentication-related behavior, and site functionality.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us with questions or requests concerning your personal information, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when our services or practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
