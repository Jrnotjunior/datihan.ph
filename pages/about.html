ABOUT DATIHAN.PH

Online first. Curated with intention.

DATIHAN.PH is an online thrift shop focused on curated pre-loved pieces, with selected pop-up events that bring the collection closer to the community.

Our story

Datihan started with a single rack at a weekend market and a simple idea: good clothes, shoes, and pants shouldn't sit unworn just because they're not new. No storefront, no overhead — just honest pricing and pieces worth digging for.

We buy, consign, and thrift pants, shirts, and shoes, then bring them to pop-ups around the city and keep the full catalog online in between. Follow along to know where we'll be next.

GOOD TO KNOW

What's your return policy?

Store credit within 5 days of pickup or delivery. Anything marked "as-is" is final sale — we'll always flag that in the listing.

What condition do you accept for consignment?

Clean, undamaged, and in-season pants, shirts, and shoes only. No stains, rips, or broken soles — light wear is fine.

How do I buy if there's no shop?

Shop the catalog here and add pieces to your cart. You'll need to sign in or create a buyer account before checkout. Cash on Delivery is available for now.

How does Cash on Delivery work?

Place your order, then pay in cash when it's delivered or handed to you at a pop-up. We'll confirm details with you over Instagram DM.

How do I get paid for a sold consignment item?

You'll get a message when something sells. Payouts are 60% of the sale price, sent within a week.
